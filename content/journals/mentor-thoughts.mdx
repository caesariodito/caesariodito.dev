This review is related to the linkedin post above, where I made some kind of "memorandum" to immortalize my mentor journey at Bangkit. I want to focus on how can I do better at that moment and giving myself some key takeaways for future journey ahead.

So, as I reflect on the journey, I think I did great on the very first phase of Bangkit and 'just enough' at the end of the journey. In the mid phase, I did create a scraper to assist me when am super busy. Its purpose is to easily monitor students' progress throughout the bangkit graduation via the bangkit dashboard website.

Why I said 'just enough' at the end of the journey? well it's because at that moment, I did so many interviews to seek a job. I also joined another program which is Apple Foundation at Surabaya and it was in the outer city where I live. Managing all of that, while maintaining relationships with mentees and the bangkit program, it has caused me a 'burnout' phase. Sometimes I just didn't have the energy to do weekly consultation sessions, and I think I yapped a lot 💀.

One thing that I noticed and wish I could do is doing the ice breaking session at weekly consultation. In my view and opinion, my mentees where divided into the "active" students and "silent'" students. and of course it's not a bad thing. It was just the unique nature of each individual of humans. But, I felt like I still can do better at that moment. I saw them that they are comfortable talking with me, but not with others, and being a mentor who spreads attention evenly, it is a very hard thing to do. Each of em have different motivations, different goals, different character, and it was pretty hard to decide what ice breaking activity that I should provide to them.

But, after all the experience that I had, I am still fully satisfied with the outcome. I didn't expect that many of them received the entre and company capstone track. Many of distinction graduates and one of them got the incubation… damn…. that's amazing. I received private message containing appreciation and thanks, and when that moment happens, man… it melt my heart.

I am so grateful that they are all my mentees and I believe each of them will encounter many more adventurous-like journey and they are ready to face that. Amazing.

---

## Key takeaways:

- Make them comfortable with each other (I tried, but can do better)
- Taking care the energy management
- Do the best, but not too much, expect nature and adapt
